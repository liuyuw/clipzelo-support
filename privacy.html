Clipzelo for iPhone

Privacy Policy

This policy describes the Clipzelo app and its support website.

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Video processing on your device

Clipzelo analyzes and compresses the videos you select on your iPhone. It does not operate a backend that receives your videos for compression. Selected media, thumbnails and metadata are used to import, estimate size, preview, compress, play and save or share your result.

On-device compression does not mean every related operation is offline. Apple's Photos picker may retrieve an iCloud-hosted video under your iPhone's network settings, and Apple services handle App Store purchases. Saving to Photos may participate in iCloud Photos if you use it. Sharing sends a copy to the app or destination you choose, under that service's own terms and privacy practices.

Photos access and optional cleanup

The system Photos picker provides access to the video you choose without requiring general read/write access to your library. Saving a new compressed copy requests permission to add to Photos.

Only if you choose optional original cleanup does Clipzelo request Photos read/write access to look up the exact original and saved copy, check the saved media and carry out an explicitly confirmed deletion. Limited or denied access can prevent cleanup. Selecting a video does not, by itself, authorize deletion.

Clipzelo uses asset identifiers when available rather than guessing from filenames or matching metadata. Session asset references are kept in memory and discarded when the session resets. It never automatically deletes an original or empties Recently Deleted. Photos and iCloud Photos manage deletion, synchronization and retention under Apple's controls.

You can review or change Clipzelo's Photos permission in iPhone Settings. Revoking permission does not itself remove copies already saved to Photos.

Temporary files and local usage records

Clipzelo creates app-local source, preview and compressed-output files while working. It removes temporary job files during cancellation, failure, starting over or launch cleanup as appropriate. Interrupted or uncooperative system work can delay cleanup. A temporary output is not permanent storage: save or share it before starting another video. Copies you save to Photos or another destination remain there until you manage them.

To enforce the Free plan, Clipzelo stores a minimal local record of successful job identifiers and calendar/clock information. It does not store video names, Photos identifiers, thumbnails or video content in that quota record. Clipzelo does not synchronize the quota across devices. Removing the app removes its app-local data under iOS's normal behavior; it does not remove Photos copies or Apple's purchase records.

The app checks available storage and file information to estimate space and validate media. It does not use those checks for advertising or tracking.

Apple-managed purchases

Lifetime Pro uses Apple's StoreKit and App Store purchase system. Clipzelo reads product information and verified transaction/entitlement information to show the current localized price, unlock Pro, restore purchases and respond to refunds or revoked transactions. It does not receive your payment-card details or create a separate Clipzelo account.

Apple processes billing, purchase history and related information according to its App Store privacy information and Privacy Policy. Restore Purchases may contact Apple and request authentication. Verified purchase information can be available locally while offline; updates such as refunds depend on Apple supplying current information.

Analytics and advertising

Clipzelo does not include advertising, third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. It does not require an app account or upload usage analytics to a developer-operated service. Apple and iOS may handle platform diagnostics and service information under your device/account settings and Apple's policies.

This support website

These static pages add no analytics, tracking pixels, forms, cookies or local-storage features, and load no third-party fonts or embedded media. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub states that it logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. Hosting is subject to the GitHub Privacy Statement; see also GitHub Pages data collection.

Support correspondence and privacy questions

If you email support, your email address, message and any attachments are received through email services and used to respond to your request. Support correspondence may remain in the support mailbox. Please send only what is needed to explain the issue; do not send private videos, Apple passwords or payment-card details.

You may contact support about app privacy or request deletion of support correspondence. Data held by Apple, GitHub, your email provider, Photos or a sharing destination is managed under their own controls and policies.

For support or privacy questions, email clipzelosupport@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

This page may be updated when Clipzelo's behavior or services change. The last-updated date above identifies the current policy.
