The basics

How to compress a video

  1. Select a video. Open Clipzelo and tap Select Video. Choose a video in the system Photos picker. Keep the app open while preparing and compressing.
  2. Choose your quality. Select High Quality, Balanced, or Small File. Sizes are estimates; actual output varies by source. Optional Preview Quality shows a real Original/Compressed sample with shared playback controls.
  3. Compress and review. Start compression, then check the actual file sizes and play the result. A short preview may not represent every scene.
  4. Save or share your copy. Tap Save Copy to Photos and wait for confirmation, or use Share. Save or share before starting another video or leaving the app; temporary results are not a permanent library.

Compression does not automatically delete your original. Optional cleanup is a separate action after saving, with verification, Photos permission and explicit confirmation.

Simple choices

Free or lifetime Pro

Free

3 successful compressions per local calendar day. All three presets are available. Failed or canceled jobs do not count. Previewing, saving, sharing and retrying a Photos save do not use another compression.

The allowance resets at local midnight. Clock or time-zone changes may delay the reset. Usage is stored locally and does not sync across devices.

Clipzelo Pro

Unlimited video compressions with a one-time purchase. No subscription or expiration. Open Upgrade to Pro on Home to see the current localized App Store price and purchase through Apple.

No app account is required. Pro access depends on a verified Apple purchase; refunds or revocation can remove access.

Already have Pro?

Restore Purchases

  1. Use the Apple Account used for the original purchase in the App Store.
  2. Open Clipzelo and tap Upgrade to Pro, then Restore Purchases.
  3. Follow any Apple sign-in prompt and wait for the result. An internet connection may be required.

If Pro is not restored, check the purchase in your Apple purchase history and confirm the App Store account. You do not need to purchase again just to troubleshoot. Contact support if the issue continues; never send your Apple password or payment details.

Troubleshooting & FAQ

A few helpful answers

Why is preparing a video taking a while?

A video may need to be retrieved from iCloud Photos before Clipzelo can analyze it. Progress can be indeterminate; a delay alone does not prove a download is happening. Check connectivity and free storage. Cancel returns you to selection, but Photos may continue its underlying transfer. Photos may use cellular under your system settings; Clipzelo does not offer a Wi-Fi-only guarantee.

Why is the output larger than expected, or not much smaller?

Estimates are approximate. Source codec, bitrate, resolution, frame rate, audio and HDR affect the result. An already efficient video may not shrink or may get larger. Try a different preset and review its actual result; smaller files can trade away visual detail. There is no guaranteed compression ratio.

What should I do about low storage or a failed compression?

Free space in iPhone Settings, close other storage-heavy tasks and retry with a disposable or shorter video to isolate the issue. Temporary source, output and Photos-save copies can require more space than the final file. Clipzelo's storage estimates are approximate. Original videos are never automatically deleted to recover space.

My compressed copy will not save to Photos.

Check available storage and Clipzelo's Photos permission in iPhone Settings. Retry saving from the existing result if available, or use Share to save elsewhere. A failed Photos save is not a saved copy. Do not delete the original unless you have reviewed a successfully saved copy.

Why is Review & Delete Original unavailable?

Cleanup requires a successfully saved and verified compressed copy, a resolvable exact original video and appropriate Photos access. Limited access may exclude that original, or the system picker may not supply its identifier. Keep both videos or review them manually in Photos; Clipzelo will not guess which asset to delete.

Does deleting the original free space immediately?

Usually it moves to Recently Deleted, and iCloud Photos may sync deletion across devices. Space may not be reclaimed immediately. To review Recently Deleted, open Photos, find Recently Deleted under Utilities, and follow Apple's unlock and deletion controls. Only permanently delete media you are sure you no longer need; Clipzelo never empties Recently Deleted.

Why can I not hear playback or see a preview?

Check device volume and audio routing, such as Bluetooth headphones. Quality comparisons start paused and muted; use full-result playback to review audio. A source may have no audio. Unsupported media or encoder settings can prevent preview/compression; keep the original and try another video. For a persistent problem, contact support with the error text.

My purchase is pending, or the App Store is unavailable.

A pending purchase, including Ask to Buy, unlocks Pro only after Apple approval and verification. Canceling a purchase does not buy Pro. If products cannot load, check connectivity, try later, or use Restore Purchases for an existing purchase. Previously completed results remain available to save or share while they remain in the app.
