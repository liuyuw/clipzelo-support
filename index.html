The basics

How to compress a video

  1. Select a video. Open Clipzelo and tap Select Video. Choose a video in the system Photos picker. Start preparation and compression with the app open. In 1.2, supported processing may continue in the background when iOS allows it; completion is not guaranteed.
  2. Choose your quality. Select High Quality, Balanced, or Small File. Sizes are estimates; actual output varies by source. Optional Preview Quality shows a real Original/Compressed sample with shared playback controls.
  3. Compress and review. Start compression, then check the actual file sizes and play the result. A short preview may not represent every scene.
  4. Save or share your copy. Tap Save Copy to Photos and wait for confirmation, or use Share. Save or share copies you want to keep. In 1.2, retained unfinished results can be reviewed after returning to the app; app-local results are not a permanent backup.

Compression does not automatically delete your original. Optional cleanup is a separate action after saving, with verification, Photos permission and explicit confirmation.

Version 1.2 guide

How to use Batch Compression

These instructions are for version 1.2. Batch is not available in earlier versions.

  1. Create a batch. Open Batch Compress, choose New Batch and select up to ten videos. Allow Photos access to the selected originals, including any limited-access selection needed for preparation and recovery.
  2. Choose one preset. The preset applies to the batch. Free selection is limited to your remaining daily allowance; three daily successful compressions are shared with single-video use. Pro removes the daily limit, but each batch still accepts at most ten videos.
  3. Connect to Wi-Fi and start. Tap Start Batch with the app open. Videos are prepared and compressed sequentially. Storage or source-availability problems can block or pause processing.
  4. Review and save. Open completed results, inspect their measured sizes and playback, then use Save to Photos and wait for confirmation. Compression does not automatically save every result to Photos. Unsaved Batch copies remain available for Manual Review until you save or explicitly discard them; they are not a permanent backup.

You can pause the queue. If Wi-Fi is lost, the current video may continue, but the next video will not start automatically. Reconnect and explicitly choose Resume. Returning to the app or restoring Wi-Fi does not automatically restart interrupted work; use the available Resume or Retry action. Both require Wi-Fi. Background processing depends on iOS and can be interrupted.

Deleting originals is optional and separate from saving. In Delete Originals, select only originals whose saved replacements have been verified, review the selection and confirm both the app and Photos prompts. A failed or uncertain deletion is not automatically retried. Saved replacements are kept; Recently Deleted can delay space reclamation. Discarding an unsaved Batch copy does not delete its Photos original.

  1. Use the Apple Account used for the original purchase in the App Store.
  2. Open Clipzelo and tap Upgrade to Pro, then Restore Purchases.

Troubleshooting & FAQ

A few helpful answers

Why is preparing a video taking a while?

A video may need to be retrieved from iCloud Photos before Clipzelo can analyze it. Progress can be indeterminate; a delay alone does not prove a download is happening. Check connectivity and free storage. Cancel returns you to selection, but Photos may continue its underlying transfer. Single-video preparation may use cellular under your system settings. In 1.2, Batch Start, Resume and Retry require an available Wi-Fi connection. Wi-Fi does not guarantee that an iCloud video is available, and Clipzelo does not control all Photos network transfers.

Will my 1.2 batch finish if I leave the app?

iOS may allow supported work to continue and show system-managed progress, but it can interrupt or decline background processing. Return to Clipzelo to inspect the queue. Interrupted work needs the available Resume or Retry action over Wi-Fi; force-quitting is not a way to keep processing. Completed unsaved results remain for Manual Review through normal relaunch, but app deletion or storage failure can remove app-local data.

Does a ten-video batch use only one Free compression?

No. In 1.2, each successful video uses one of the same three daily Free compressions used by single-video mode. Check your remaining allowance before starting. Preparing, failing or canceling a job does not count as a successful compression, and saving or deleting an original does not spend another slot.

Why is the output larger than expected, or not much smaller?

Estimates are approximate. Source codec, bitrate, resolution, frame rate, audio and HDR affect the result. An already efficient video may not shrink or may get larger. Try a different preset and review its actual result; smaller files can trade away visual detail. There is no guaranteed compression ratio.

What should I do about low storage or a failed compression?

Free space in iPhone Settings, close other storage-heavy tasks and retry with a disposable or shorter video to isolate the issue. Temporary source, output and Photos-save copies can require more space than the final file. Clipzelo's storage estimates are approximate. Original videos are never automatically deleted to recover space.

My compressed copy will not save to Photos.

Check available storage and Clipzelo's Photos permission in iPhone Settings. Retry saving from the existing result if available, or use Share to save elsewhere. A failed Photos save is not a saved copy. Do not delete the original unless you have reviewed a successfully saved copy.

Why is Review & Delete Original unavailable?

Cleanup requires a successfully saved and verified compressed copy, a resolvable exact original video and appropriate Photos access. Limited access may exclude that original, or the system picker may not supply its identifier. Keep both videos or review them manually in Photos; Clipzelo will not guess which asset to delete.

Does deleting the original free space immediately?

Usually it moves to Recently Deleted, and iCloud Photos may sync deletion across devices. Space may not be reclaimed immediately. To review Recently Deleted, open Photos, find Recently Deleted under Utilities, and follow Apple's unlock and deletion controls. Only permanently delete media you are sure you no longer need; Clipzelo never empties Recently Deleted.

Why can I not hear playback or see a preview?

Check device volume and audio routing, such as Bluetooth headphones. Quality comparisons start paused and muted; use full-result playback to review audio. A source may have no audio. Unsupported media or encoder settings can prevent preview/compression; keep the original and try another video. For a persistent problem, contact support with the error text.

My purchase is pending, or the App Store is unavailable.

A pending purchase, including Ask to Buy, unlocks Pro only after Apple approval and verification. Canceling a purchase does not buy Pro. If products cannot load, check connectivity, try later, or use Restore Purchases for an existing purchase. Previously completed results remain available to save or share while they remain in the app.
